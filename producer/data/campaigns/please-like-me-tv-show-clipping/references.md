ABOUT THE SHOW
Please Like Me is an award-winning Australian comedy created by and starring Josh Thomas, based on his own life. It follows a 20-something guy who figures out he’s gay, stumbles into adulthood with his best friends, and looks after his mum through her mental health struggles. It’s sweet, sharp and very funny.
