	WHO TORI KELLY IS
Read this once. It will make your hooks better.
• She voiced Meena the elephant in the movie Sing (2016) and Sing 2 (2021). Most people know her voice from that and do not realise it.
• She has two Grammys, both from 2019, for the album Hiding Place: Best Gospel Album and Best Gospel Performance or Song for Never Alone with Kirk Franklin.
• She started by posting videos to YouTube from her bedroom, and she did not make the Top 24 on American Idol in 2010.
• Her breakout single was Nobody Love in 2015, from the album Unbreakable Smile.
• In July 2023 she collapsed while out to dinner and was treated for blood clots in her legs and lungs. She recovered and went back out on tour.
• She married Andre Murillo in 2018. Their first child, a son named Zayden, was born in November 2025. She is a new mom on this record.
• Her new album is God Must Really Love Me, released 12 June 2026 on Epic Records. It is about her faith and about becoming a parent.
• At the Tiny Desk she played a toy glockenspiel and told the room she took it from her son's bedroom.
