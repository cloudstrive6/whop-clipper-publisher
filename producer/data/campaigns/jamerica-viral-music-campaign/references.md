Jamerica, Never Can Say Goodbye — Campaign Brief
Campaign: "Never Can Say Goodbye" audio push — Instagram Reels / TikTok
Client tag: @jamericanproductions
Theme: Heartbreak, lost love and nostalgia
Overview
Creators make short-form videos using the "Never Can Say Goodbye" sound, starting at the vocal hook. Content should lean into heartbreak, loss and nostalgia so the emotion of the track carries the video.
Sound
Instagram audio (must be tagged in the Reel): Instagram Reels audio
TikTok sound: TikTok sound link
Posting Requirements
Every submission must meet all of these. Posts that miss any requirement won't be approved.
Audio starts at 0:53 — landing on the "Never can say goodbye" vocals, and carries the full vocals
Minimum video length: 15 seconds
Audio at 100% volume
Song tagged in the video using the official audio link above
Tag @jamericanproductions in the caption
Hashtags: #dancehall #jamaica #reggae
Post location set to Kingston, Jamaica
Atleast 0.5% required
40% T1 audience required
Audience: Jamaican diaspora in North America — top metros NYC, New Jersey, Connecticut, South Florida/Miami, Atlanta, LA. Not Jamaica directly.
Content Guidelines
What we want to see:
Heartbreak and lost love — breakups, the one that got away, missing someone
Memes about loss in a wider sense — losing a favourite thing, a moment, a friend, an era
Nostalgic formats that trade on recognising the original — throwbacks, "remember when", classic format callbacks
Let the hook do the work: time the emotional beat or punchline of the video to hit right as "Never can say goodbye" comes in.
