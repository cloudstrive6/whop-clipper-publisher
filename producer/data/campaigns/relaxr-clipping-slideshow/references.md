﻿CAMPAIGN BRIEF
RELAXR
Campaign Overview
Niche
	Wellness / Sleep / Meditation
	Format
	TikTok Slideshows
	Platforms
	TikTok
	

Critical Rules
* Tier 1 views only
* Reference-style content follows standard approval
* Custom concepts and CTAs are only approved if they show strong performance (10,000+ views)


1. Account Setup
Account Style
* Use calming, aesthetic wellness / self-care photos (Pinterest or AI-generated)
* Make the page look like a real wellness / self-care / meditation account
* Stay on niche at all times (sleep, calm, meditation, wellness)
Username Examples
Use a random name + wellness keyword:
* calmwithava
* sleepwellmaya
* emilymeditation
* vanessawellness


2. Account Warm-Up
Before posting Relaxr content:
* Warm up the account for 3 full days
* Watch wellness / sleep / meditation / self-care content
* Like posts
* Follow creators
* Leave comments
* Engage naturally


3. Content Rules
Images
✅ Allowed (but not exclusively): AI-generated images, Pinterest images, Instagram images.
❌ Not Allowed: Random screenshots, stock photos
Sounds
* Use trending calm / wellness / aesthetic sounds
* Easiest method: use sounds from the reference accounts
Hashtags (Examples)
Include something like these on every post: #relaxr #meditation #selfcare
On-Screen Text
✅ Use TikTok’s native text tool only
❌ Do NOT:
* Burn text into images
* Use CapCut text overlays
* Use external text overlays


4. Content Format (Most Important)
Every slideshow should be a wellness / sleep / meditation tip or routine.
Example Hooks
* How to meditate (for beginners)
* This is how you are becoming UNRECOGNIZABLE in 30 days.
* Things that don’t look like meditation
* 5 ways to actually wind down after work
* How to stop your thoughts spiraling at night
* How to romanticize your night routine
Follow Reference Videos
Match:
* Hook style
* First slide format
* Text structure
* Slide order
* Pacing
* Overall content style
Keep it simple. The text provides the value. The images support the text.


5. Relaxr CTA Slide (Required)
Every slideshow must end with a Relaxr CTA slide. 
CTA Style — Sticker CTA
* Place the Relaxr lotus sticker on a calming photo (beach, sky, bed, etc.) with a “Relaxr app” label above it (use the approved sticker asset).
* Add an app screenshot on the slide (also in the approved assets)
* Add “Search Relaxr on the App store” on the slide
Reference example:
“Use your phone to process your emotions. Old wounds resurface when ignored. My therapist recommended the app Relaxr. It’s been so helpful for my mental health ❤”
CTA Copy — Examples Only (don’t use rigidly)
These are just to show the tone. — write your own line that:
* Feels natural
* Matches the slideshow topic
* Fits the wellness / sleep niche
* Has viral potential
Examples:
* My therapist recommended Relaxr for winding down at night — it’s helped my sleep and my mental health so much. (I use it every night before bed.)
* Bonus tip: give your racing thoughts somewhere to go before bed. I use Relaxr to wind down and actually fall asleep. (It’s been a game changer for my sleep.)
* Use a wind-down routine to calm your mind before sleep — I use Relaxr for guided meditations and sleep sounds. (It’s part of my nightly routine now.)


6. Comment Replies
Common question: “What app is this?”
Reply naturally (with the image CTA):
* Relaxr, this one
* I use Relaxr
Keep responses short and human.
If viewers leave comments that could hurt conversions, delete them. Specifically:
* Delete comments saying the video is an ad/promo
* Delete comments saying the account is a bot or fake
* Delete comments saying the app costs money


7. Traffic Requirements
Tier 1 traffic qualifies:
* United States
* United Kingdom
* Canada
* Australia
VPNs are allowed.


Final Checklist
Before posting, confirm:
* ✅ Account warmed up for 3 days
* ✅ Pinterest, AI, or Instagram images
* ✅ Trending wellness / calm sound
* ✅ #relaxr #meditation #selfcare type hashtags
* ✅ Native TikTok text only
* ✅ Strong first-slide hook
* ✅ Followed reference account / video style
* ✅ CTA slide added (Phone OR Sticker)
