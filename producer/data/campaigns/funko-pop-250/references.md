THE MISSION
POP!250 celebrates the icons who shaped 250 years of American pop culture, and Funko is auctioning ONE-OF-ONE Pops of them, live.
One piece of each. One winning bidder. Real money on the line, live on eBay Live and run by Goldin, the king of collectibles. Your job: clip it like the auctions that break the internet, like Banksy shredding itself at Sotheby’s or Logan Paul’s $16.5M Pikachu card at Goldin. Program details: funko.com/pop-250
	

1
	The Stream
	

📺 VOD here: Goldin × Funko POP!250 on eBay Live
🗓️ Wednesday, September 23 · 6:00 PM to ~8:00 PM EST
🏆 Auction house: Goldin (@goldinco), the king of collectibles. The same house that sold Logan Paul’s Pikachu Illustrator for a record $16.5M. When Goldin runs the auction, the prices are real.
🎙️ Hosts: Ken Goldin (Goldin founder & CEO, @kengoldin), Kyle Brandt (Television personality, @kylebrandt), and expert co-host Alex Fung (Goldin Pop Culture Consignment Director, @iphone.elite)
The format: a decade-by-decade run through 250 years of American icons, from the 1770s to the 2020s. One-of-one auctions, live giveaways, and big reveals. Clip live, and sweep the VOD after.
	

⭐ THE LINEUP (one-of-one Pops on the night):
Auctions: Benjamin Franklin, George Washington, Davy Crockett, Edgar Allan Poe, Mark Twain, Annie Oakley, Harry Houdini, Charlie Chaplin, Babe Ruth, Jackie Robinson, Lucille Ball, Tina Turner, Bruce Lee
Giveaways: Sacagawea, Frederick Douglass, Amelia Earhart, P.T. Barnum, Buffalo Bill, and more
Big reveals: Bruce Lee, Babe Ruth, Jackie Robinson, George Washington, Lucie Ball, Houdini, Chaplin.
Know the names before the stream. Focus on the big moments, the crazy prices and the exclusive reveals.

3
	The Rules (Funko First, Always)
	

⚠️ THREE RULES, EVERY CLIP:
1. A Pop is IN every clip. The figure on screen. No Pop in frame = no payout.
2. Every clip is ABOUT Pops. The Pop, the auction of the Pop, the icon behind the Pop. Captions and on-screen text stay Pop-related too.
3. This campaign is for FUNKO. Hype Funko and POP!250. Goldin and the hosts are part of the story (it’s their auction, and the Goldin name is what makes the prices mean something), but the star of every clip is the Pop, never the platform. Clips framed around eBay Live or Goldin instead of the Pop get rejected.
	

4
	Prices Are The Content
	

✅ Real prices are your best hook. “omg this one of one pop just went for $5,000” is a perfect caption. Use the REAL numbers as they happen: the bids, the jumps, the final hammer.
❌ No clickbait. Never invent, inflate, or round up a number. The real prices are wild enough.
📚 Context for your hooks (collectibles sell for INSANE money): the 15 most expensive Pops ever  ·  the $100K Willy Wonka Golden Ticket Pop  ·  Logan Paul’s $16.5M Pikachu at Goldin
	

5
	What To Clip
	

•  The big names first. MrBeast, Michael Jordan, Britney Spears, Michael Jackson, Babe Ruth, Jackie Robinson. Whether it’s a reveal or an auction, any moment with these six is a clip that can go big on its own.
•  The hammer moments. Final calls, last-second bids, the number landing. The jaw-drop when the price hits is what made Banksy at Sotheby’s and Logan Paul’s Pikachu go viral. Same energy here.
•  The bidding wars. Two bidders trading jumps on a one-of-one. Build the tension, then pay it off with the final number.
•  The reveals. The first look at each one-of-one, especially the big names. Clip the reaction along with the Pop.
•  The host moments. Ken Goldin, Kyle Brandt, and Alex Fung reacting as the bids climb. Their reactions are clips.
•  The icon stories. Lead with the big names and the sports legends. The 30-second story of why Jordan, Babe Ruth, or Jackie Robinson matters + the Pop + the price = a complete video.
•  The giveaways. Trivia moments, winner reveals, the chaos of free one-of-ones. Clippable, but lower priority than the big names.

8
	How To Edit
	

1. Hook in 2 seconds: the price, the big name, or the final call first.
2. Big on-screen captions, Pop-related, readable on a phone.
3. Vertical 9:16, tight pacing. Cut the dead air between bids.
4. Celebrate the icons. This is a celebration of 250 years of pop culture. No controversy-bait edits of the legends being honored.
5. Make each clip different. No copy-paste spam.
9
	Payouts
	

How payouts work
Approved clips pay automatically through Content Rewards per qualified view, with a per-clip cap and a $1 minimum. Every clip gets manual review and bot screening before payout. Current rates, caps, and audience requirements are on the Whop campaign page.
