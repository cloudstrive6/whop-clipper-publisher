﻿DiegoLoveless Gaming Clipping: Full Rules Brief
by ClipLaunch 🚀


Campaign Overview + Goal 🎯


Who is DiegoLoveless? Diego Loveless is a popular online content creator and gamer, primarily known for his entertaining streams and high-level gameplay in the team shooter Marvel Rivals. He streams under the handle @diegoloveless on Twitch and TikTok
He is the kind of creator who should have millions of eyes on him and the goal of this campaign is to make sure that happens.


Your Mission: Clip his best GAMING stream moments and push the narrative that Diego is wildly underrated. 


Where to clip from: All clips must come exclusively from Diego’s GAMING Twitch VODs. You can find them here: 


DIEGO'S TWITCH (GAMING VODS ONLY)




Pay Rate + Budget 💰


* CPM Rate: $1.5 per 1,000 views on approved clips
* Budget: $2,100 total campaign budget
* Minimum Views: 3,000 views required before submitting analytics for approval
* Max Payout: $300 per clipper for the duration of this campaign
* Approval: only approved clips count toward payouts. Check approval process for how to get approved.


Rules 📋


* Dedicated page requirement: NONE
* Bio Requirement: NONE  
* Source content: you must clip exclusively from Diego's gaming clips on Twitch under the clips tab (ONLY Gaming Clips ALLOWED): DIEGO'S TWITCH (USE GAMING CLIPS ONLY)
* On screen/caption requirement: Must say “twitch.tv/diegoloveless” either on screen or in the caption
See image on how to source clips (head to https://www.twitch.tv/diegoloveless/clips?range=7d and download the clips):


   * Tier 1 audience: 40%+ Tier 1 (United States, United Kingdom, Canada, Australia) audience is required for payout. Check your analytics before submitting.




Assets Required 📁

Submission Checklist ✅


   * ✅ Clip is sourced exclusively from a DiegoLoveless GAMING Twitch VODs ONLY
   * ✅ Required overlay is applied and visible throughout the entire clip.
   * ✅ Clip has reached at least 3,000 views before submitting analytics.
   * ✅ Analytics screen recording shows Video Analytics > Viewers > Locations scrolled from top to bottom for the specific video.
   * ✅ Clip was submitted to the campaign portal within 30 minutes of posting.
   * ✅ Account has 40%+ Tier 1 audience confirmed in analytics before submitting.
   * ✅ Clip does not look like a paid ad or a sponsored post.
   * ✅ All engagement on the clip is real and organic, with no botted or fake activity of any kind.
   * ✅ Payout total for this campaign has not yet reached the $150 per clipper cap.


What to Avoid 🚫


   * ❌ Clipping from any content other than Diego's Gaming Twitch VODs
   * ❌ Content that feels like an ad. Scripted, promotional, or overly polished clips will be rejected.
   * ❌ Missing or incomplete analytics. Showing the wrong video, showing account wide stats, or cutting off the screen recording early will result in rejection.
   * ❌ Submitting before hitting 3,000 views. Wait until your clip reaches the minimum before submitting for review.
   * ❌ Submitting more than 30 minutes after posting. Late submissions are ineligible regardless of performance.
   * ❌ Clips with less than 50% Tier 1 audience. Tier 3 heavy audiences will not be approved for payout.
   * ❌ Botted or fake engagement of any kind. Purchased or artificially inflated views, likes, comments, or follows result in an immediate ban and a report to the Whop team.
   * ❌ Multi accounting. Using more than one account to participate will result in disqualification and a permanent ban.
   * ❌ Exceeding the $300 per clipper cap. Once you hit the cap, additional clips in this campaign do not count toward payouts.


Approval Process 🔍


Submit each video to the campaign within 30 minutes of posting. Once a moderator announces in the chat that they are reviewing and approving videos, submit your screen recording of analytics through support chat, but only after your video has reached the minimum view count required for that specific campaign. Analytics must be for the specific video, not the whole account: go to Video Analytics > Viewers, then scroll to Locations, and show every section from top to bottom.


Expected turnaround: submissions are checked daily. If there is an issue with your screen recording or analytics, our team will notify you. Approval means your clip qualifies and counts toward your campaign earnings; rejection means it does not count (common reasons: it looks like an ad, Tier 3 audience, banned words, or missing or incomplete analytics). Payments are processed automatically through Content Rewards via Whop and can take up to 7 days to arrive. All clips are subject to review. Only approved clips count toward campaign payouts.


Need Help? 💬


Stuck on something or not sure if your clip fits the brief? Reach out and ask any questions in the ClipLaunch Chat and we will get back as soon as possible. We are active 24/7 with support!
