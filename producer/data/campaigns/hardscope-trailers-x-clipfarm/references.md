3
	How to Edit
	

1. Clip any moment from any of the three trailers. Show HardScope talent with other big talent when you can.
2. Hook in the first 2 seconds.
3. Add big, readable subtitles. Every word should be easy to follow with the sound off.
4. Keep it vertical and fast-paced. 9:16, no dead air, and at least 7 seconds long.
5. Trailer footage only. Never mix in footage or audio from anywhere else, and never upload a full trailer.
