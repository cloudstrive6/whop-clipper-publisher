1. Eligibility
You confirm that you are at least 18 years old, that you are accepting for yourself and not on behalf of anyone else, and that the information you give us is accurate. One person, one account. You may not share your access with anyone.
2. What is confidential
"Confidential Information" means everything you see or learn through this campaign that isn't already public, including:
* (a) the identity of the Client Parties, the podcast owner, or anyone else behind this campaign, and the fact that they are running a clipping campaign at all;
* (b) the campaign brief, guidelines, strategy, and any messages from us;
* (c) the campaign dashboard and everything on it, including budgets, spend, reward rates, CPMs, payouts, leaderboards, view counts, and other creators' results;
* (d) any unreleased or pre-release content, such as episodes, clips, audio, video, artwork, and release dates;
* (e) any non-public information about the host, guests, or their teams or families, including contact details, schedules, travel, and locations ("Personal Information");
* (f) the contents of any private channel, server, folder, or link we give you access to.
Information is not confidential if it is already public through no fault of yours or anyone else in the campaign. Publicly released podcast episodes are public, but the information in (a)–(c) stays confidential even if the episodes are public. Unreleased content stays confidential until its official public release, even if it leaks.
3. What you may do
You may clip, edit, and post content from publicly released episodes only, on the platforms listed in the brief, following the brief. That is the only permitted use of campaign content. You may use standard editing, clipping, and captioning tools on publicly released episodes, including AI-assisted ones.
4. What you must not do
You must not:
1. screenshot, screen-record, photograph, copy, or share the dashboard, brief, payouts, leaderboards, or any private channel — publicly or privately, including in other Discords, group chats, or "clipping tips" content;
2. say, hint, or tag that the podcast, its host, any Client Party, or any company is running or paying for a campaign, or name any Client Party, except for any disclosure required by Section 6;
3. tag, mention, or @ the podcast, its host, or its guests in any post unless the brief expressly allows it;
4. post or share your earnings, rates, or CPM for this campaign;
5. share invite links, restricted links, logins, or assets with anyone;
6. post, share, or preview unreleased content, or post anything before the date the brief allows;
7. put any Confidential Information into any AI tool, chatbot, or assistant. This includes the brief, payout information, dashboard data, messages from us, and unreleased content. Only publicly released episodes may go into editing, clipping, or captioning tools under Section 3;
8. use AI or any other method to clone the host's or any guest's voice, create deepfakes, create digital replicas, or generate synthetic speech, faces, or statements, or use anyone's name, likeness, voice, or content to train an AI model;
9. edit clips so they mislead, take statements out of context in a way that changes their meaning, or are defamatory, hateful, sexual, or illegal;
10. use campaign content outside this campaign, including for other campaigns, your own channels' promotion, or resale;
11. use purchased views, bots, engagement pods, or any artificial engagement;
12. share or use any Personal Information for any reason;
13. identify Project A&R, any Client Party, the podcast, or the host as a client or partner of yours in any portfolio, pitch, or post.

You must follow the brief. We may change the brief at any time, and changes apply from when we post them. We may reject, not pay for, or require removal of any clip.
6. Disclosure
You are responsible for following the platform rules that apply to you. If the brief sets out a disclosure method, use it. Any disclosure must not name a Client Party unless the brief says so.
7. Leaks and takedowns
* (a) If you learn that any Confidential Information has leaked or been misused, including by another creator, tell us through the campaign's private support chat within 12 hours.
* (b) If we ask you to remove a post, remove it within 12 hours of our request.
* (c) You will help us identify the source of any leak and cooperate with takedown and enforcement efforts, including by any Client Party.

Your participation ends when the campaign ends, when you leave, or when we remove you. Within 5 days of the end you must delete all campaign files, downloads, and notes you hold (other than clips already posted publicly as permitted), and on request confirm in writing that you have done so.
