	What to Post (Three Formats)
	

A. The "what is this?" reveal. Open on the biggest face or wildest shot in the trailer with text like "wait, what is this?" or "a new channel with ALL these creators?!" Build the intrigue and don't explain everything. The viewer should leave wanting to know more.
B. Creator roll call. Fast cuts of each creator in the trailer with their name on screen: Neon, Zach Justice, MatCrackz, one channel. The stacked names are the hook.
C. Show tease. Pick one show from the trailer and hype that alone, like "Neon has a new show??" Add that show's hashtag.
It should feel like the biggest news in the creator space this week. Use your own words.
	

4
	How to Edit
	

1. Hook in the first 2 seconds. Lead with the biggest face or wildest shot, not a slow build.
2. Names on screen. Viewers need to know who they're looking at, fast.
3. Tease, don't explain. Leave them asking what HardScope is.
4. Add big, readable subtitles. Every word should be easy to follow with the sound off.
5. Keep it vertical and fast-paced. 9:16, no dead air, and at least 7 seconds long.
6. Trailer footage only. Never mix in footage or audio from anywhere else, and never upload the full trailer.
