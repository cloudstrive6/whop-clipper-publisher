HOW TO MAKE YOUR CLIPS HIT
Hook in under 1 second.
Do not start with:
"So, yeah..."
 "That's interesting because..."
 "I think..."
Cut directly into the strongest sentence.
* Contrarian Hook
Start with the statement that makes someone stop scrolling.
"Stop putting this on your skin."
Then provide the context.
* Curiosity Hook
Create an information gap:
"This is the first thing Austin changed when he started living healthier..."
* Everyday-Exposure Hook
Connect the topic to something viewers encounter constantly:
"You use this every single day."
"Most people never think about what's actually in their shower."
* Brendan + Austin Hook
Their friendship is an asset.
When both appear, use framing such as:
"Organic Tarzan and Santa Cruz Medicinals agree on one thing..."
or simply allow a strong disagreement/conversation to play naturally.
Hook → context → payoff.
