REQUIREMENTS

Before you post your first clip, review these requirements. Videos that don't meet every requirement won't be approved for payout.

Content may be sourced from any approved video, stream, clip, or social media post featuring Pumpfun or one of the approved Pumpfun creators.
Videos must include the official Pumpfun watermark. You can find the watermark here: https://drive.google.com/file/d/1HXBgU9ZuAVR0AkxntS_Tm7q6VJjkaWsX/view?usp=sharing
Participating accounts must include "https://pump.fun/" in bio to be eligible for rewards.
Submitted videos must clearly feature Pumpfun, an approved Pumpfun creator, the Pumpfun ecosystem, the Pumpfun mobile app, or the Pumpfun trading terminal.
Content may include clips from approximately 20 approved creators, including lifestyle creators producing content around Pumpfun and streamers using the Pumpfun terminal to trade.
Videos can be posted on Instagram Reels, TikTok, and YouTube Shorts.
Content may also be posted on X when approved. X submissions should come from established accounts with real distribution or dedicated accounts capable of reaching the crypto and trading audience.
Instagram and TikTok are the primary platforms for this campaign.
Audience must be at least 50% native English-speaking countries (USA, CA, AU, UK, NZ).
An account's usual audience may not be in a vastly different niche. For example, posting Pumpfun clips on a bird-watching profile may make your video subject to rejection.
Existing accounts should ideally be focused on crypto, trading, finance, entrepreneurship, lifestyle, streaming, or closely related content.
Your captions must use the hashtag #Pumpfun
Payouts start once your video hits the Minimum View Requirement and is approved.
Clips must provide enough context for viewers to understand the moment, product, creator, or discussion being featured.
approved campaign link must be in the clipper’s bio while participating.
Content should help distribute Pumpfun-related moments while remaining entertaining, informative, or engaging as a standalone post.
All videos will receive an end-of-campaign review for payment-revocation
Videos cannot portray Pumpfun or its approved creators in a negative light.
Videos cannot contain false, misleading, or deceptive claims about Pumpfun, trading results, financial returns, or the Pumpfun ecosystem.
Do not present trading outcomes or profits as guaranteed.
Views and engagement cannot be manipulated in any way. This includes botting, repost manipulation, collab posts, artificial engagement, or any other attempt to inflate performance.
Explicitly prohibit reused/recycled/AI generated clips, spam uploads, and static/no-edit content.
official watermark fully visible throughout. Prohibit other campaign watermarks.
Do not submit unrelated crypto or trading content that does not clearly feature Pumpfun, an approved Pumpfun creator, or a Pumpfun product.

GET PAID

All payouts run through Whop's Content Rewards. Here's the flow from posting to cash-out.

OPEN THE CONTENT REWARDS APP
You'll find it inside the Whop Community. This is where you submit your clip links and track approval status.
VERIFY YOUR WHOP ACCOUNT
Before you can be paid, you need to verify your account by connecting your socials. Go to Whop → Settings → Connected Accounts.
REVIEW THE APPROVED CREATOR LIST
Before clipping, confirm that the creator, stream, video, or social media post is approved for the Pumpfun campaign.
CHOOSE THE RIGHT CONTENT
Select content featuring an approved Pumpfun creator, the Pumpfun ecosystem, the mobile app, or a streamer using the Pumpfun terminal.
SUBMIT YOUR VIDEO LINKS
Once verified, head back to Content Rewards and paste the link to every Pumpfun clip you've posted. Submit within 30 minutes of posting.
HIT MVR AND GET APPROVED
Once your video crosses the Minimum View Requirement and an approver signs off, your payout queues up automatically.
KEEP THE POST LIVE
Approved posts must remain public and accessible while views are being tracked and until the payout process is complete.

Questions? Open a support ticket in the Reach Discord. For Whop-specific issues such as payouts or account verification, contact Whop support.

ACCOUNT CREATION

Dedicated accounts are not required. If you have a pre-existing clip-focused account, crypto page, trading page, finance page, creator page, or another account with a closely related audience, you can skip account creation.

The campaign will use a mix of new accounts dedicated to Pumpfun and approved creators, along with existing accounts that already have relevant distribution. If you don't already have a clipping-related page with 100+ followers, we strongly recommend setting up a dedicated clipping account.

CREATE FRESH ACCOUNTS
Make new accounts on TikTok, Instagram, and YouTube. You may also create an X account if you plan to participate in approved X distribution. If you're outside the USA, see the USA targeting section first — that workflow determines how you set these up.
PICK A CAMPAIGN-RELATED USERNAME
Use a name that relates to Pumpfun, an approved Pumpfun creator, crypto, or a similar trading niche. Examples: PumpfunClips, PumpfunDaily, PumpfunMoments, PumpfunReels, [CreatorName]Clips, or [CreatorName]Daily.
SET A PUMPFUN-RELATED PROFILE PICTURE
Use the campaign thumbnail, a Pumpfun-themed image, approved creator artwork, or official Pumpfun artwork.
BUILD A RELEVANT PROFILE
Your bio, username, profile picture, and surrounding posts should make it clear that the account focuses on Pumpfun, crypto, trading, finance, or the featured creator.
KEEP THE ACCOUNT CONSISTENT
Avoid mixing Pumpfun content with completely unrelated niches. Consistent accounts are more likely to reach the correct audience and be approved.
USE EXISTING DISTRIBUTION WHEN AVAILABLE
If you already operate an established crypto, trading, finance, or creator-focused account with authentic reach, you may use it for this campaign.
X ACCOUNT STANDARDS
X is most valuable when the account already has real distribution in the crypto or trading community. Low-quality repost accounts or accounts without a relevant audience may not be approved for X submissions.
TARGETING USA

To hit the audience requirement, make sure your content reads as US-targeted.

Use English-language captions and on-screen text.
Avoid country-specific slang from outside the target regions.
Tag locations as US cities when relevant.
Use hooks, terminology, topics, and references that make sense to English-speaking crypto and trading audiences.
Post during hours that give your content the best opportunity to reach viewers in the USA and other approved English-speaking countries.
If you have access to creator-side audience insights, check that your historical audience is majority US/UK/CA/AU/NZ before joining.
Be prepared to provide audience analytics if requested during the review process.
Accounts with a historical audience concentrated outside the approved countries may be rejected even if an individual post performs well.
FINAL RULES
Meet every quality standard listed in the Requirements article.
Only clip approved Pumpfun creators, streams, videos, products, and social media content.
Make sure Pumpfun, an approved Pumpfun creator, the Pumpfun ecosystem, the mobile app, or the trading terminal is clearly featured.
Prioritize distribution on Instagram Reels and TikTok.
YouTube Shorts remains approved for this campaign.
Only use X when your account has relevant, authentic distribution or when your X account has been approved for the campaign.
Use #Pumpfun in every caption.
Submit Pumpfun clips within 30 minutes of posting.
Keep submitted posts public and accessible during the review and payout process.
Read every section of this page before posting your first clip.
Do not change the caption, delete the post, archive the post, or make the account private while the submission is being reviewed.
Do not misrepresent Pumpfun, its products, its creators, or potential trading outcomes.

One strike and you're out. Botting views, AI-generated content, artificial engagement, or any attempt to game the system removes you from every Reach campaign permanently.
