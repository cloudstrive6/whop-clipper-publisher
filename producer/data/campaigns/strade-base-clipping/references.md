﻿STRADE BASE
Clipping Campaign Guidelines & Posting Requirements

Campaign Overview:
Strade Base is a music investment platform that lets fans and investors buy into shares of artists’ royalties like a stock exchange.
The goal: Grow both sides of the Strade Base marketplace by attracting emerging artists into Strade Discovery while building demand from fans and potential investors. Artist acquisition should be a major focus, showing artists how they can submit music directly to DJs for playlist, radio and live-event consideration, build early fan demand through Strade Requests, and potentially turn that demand into funding for their careers.

  CREATIVE 3: SOMEONE JUST BACKED YOUR MUSIC  
Concept
	This concept targets upcoming African artists by showing how Strade can turn fan support into real capital they can put back into their careers. The hook should make the money feel tangible in local terms, for example, £1,000 is roughly ₦1.8M in Nigeria, which can suddenly mean real budget for studio time, visuals, promo, travel, or shows. The content should frame Strade as a way for artists to potentially access meaningful funding without waiting on a label or relying only on streaming income.
	Source
	Source upcoming African artist content that shows the grind and growth: studio sessions, DIY music video shoots, small performances, rehearsals, travel, and behind-the-scenes clips. Prioritize footage that feels ambitious and authentic, then pair it with stronger payoff visuals like bigger shows, better production, or celebration moments to show what added funding could unlock.
	TOS Examples
	“POV: your fans invested £1,000 into your music and now you can actually fund ur next rollout”|

“POV: you go from funding everything yourself to having real money behind your next release w/o a label”

“Imagine being an upcoming artist and someone puts ₦2M behind your career every week”

“Independent African artists might have found a new way to fund their music through Strade Base” “It’s pretty much robinhood for music” 

“POV: your next studio session, video and promo are covered because somebody invested in your music on Strade Base”

Non-Negotiable Approval Rules
EVERY POST MUST:
* Tag Strade naturally in the caption
* Use one of the approved creative angles above
* Be posted to TikTok or Instagram Reels
* Come from / target an account with a majority audience in the UK, approved Tier 1 markets, or approved African markets
* Feel like organic content — not a traditional advertisement
* Use original clips that aren’t stolen from other clippers 
* Remain public for the required campaign period
	





Automatic Rejection
Posts will be rejected if:
* @stradebase is not tagged in the caption 
* The post looks like a blatant advertisement
* Uses an artist who is not on the strade base roster
* The creative does not follow one of the approved angles
* Content is stolen or contains another page's watermark
* Views or engagement are manipulated
* The same edit is spammed across multiple accounts without meaningful variation
* Audience geography does not fit the campaign
