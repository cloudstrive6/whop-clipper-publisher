ABOUT EMPIRE OF THE SUN
Australian electronic duo, four top ten albums, over 7.5 billion career streams. They are midway through the Ask That God: Afterlife world tour, their biggest run ever: 51 shows across 18 countries in 2026, including Lollapalooza, Red Rocks and the Hollywood Bowl. 1. Pull footage from the approved Dropbox folder. Nothing else.

3. Add on-screen text from the bank below, or your own variation.
4. Post to TikTok or Instagram Reels with the campaign hashtags.
5. Once you pass 2,000 views, submit your video analytics.
