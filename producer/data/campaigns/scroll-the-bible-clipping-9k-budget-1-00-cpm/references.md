Scroll The Bible — Campaign Brief
Everything you need to clip for this campaign. Read the whole doc before posting.


1. Guidelines & Requirements
* Clippers must have a dedicated page on corresponding platforms just for Scroll The Bible.
* Clips can't make Scroll The Bible look bad (captions, editing, title).
* Clips/engagement can't be botted in any way.
* The minimum duration of the clips must be 10 seconds.
* Clips can only be posted on Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube.
* Clips can only target USA, CA, AU, UK, NZ (English speaking countries) (50%+).
* Payouts start every hour once your video hits MVR & gets approved, so check the Content Rewards channel.
* You must always have a call to action (eg. app link in bio).
* AI clips are not allowed. Phonk music is not allowed.
* Negative comments about pricing must be deleted.
* Clips must have a minimum engagement rate of 1% in likes relative to total views.
* Clips must keep the spoken CTA where the speaker mentions the app (eg. “my friend created an app that lets you scroll the Bible, it’s called Scroll The Bible on the App Store”). Do not trim it out. Clips that remove it won’t be approved.
* You must comment on every clip you post with the comment below, then pin it:
This app is called Scroll The Bible, available on the App Store.
Payouts are subject to available campaign funds at the time of approval. If the budget has been exhausted before your submission is reviewed, compensation cannot be guaranteed.
2. Account Creation
Set your dedicated Scroll The Bible pages up like this before posting your first clip:
Step 1: Create the accounts
* Create brand new accounts on TikTok, Instagram, and YouTube.
Step 2: Pick a username
* Set the name to something centered around Christianity.
Examples:
* Grace Daily, Faithful Feed, Faith Fuel, Daily Bread, Chosen Daily, Bible Daily, Scripture Sips, Sunday Soul, Blessed Daily, Kingdom Minded, Holy Habits, Faith First, Redeemed Daily, Grace Notes, Daily Devotion, Prayer Daily, Verse Daily, Faithful Daily, Blessed Feed, Gospel Daily
Tip: if you're struggling to find a username, ask ChatGPT for ideas.
Step 3: Profile picture
* AI-generate a profile picture based on your username (no words).
Step 4: Bio
* Mention Scroll The Bible's main account in your bio:
⇊ Scroll The Bible App ⇊
App Store | https://apps.apple.com/us/app/scroll-the-bible/id6756558351
Step 5: Warm up your account
* LASTLY, you NEED to warm up your account. This is so you don't start off getting 0 views.
* Wait 24-48 hours before uploading any videos. During these 24-48 hours, act like a normal user by following people on your FYP, as well as liking and commenting on videos.
* Post at least one daily story (just reupload your videos on your story). This is super important and has been helping accounts go viral much more consistently.

1. Campaign Overview
Scroll The Bible makes faith feel native to the feed: short, scroll-stopping clips built from scripture and everyday encouragement. This campaign runs on reach. We flood Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube with a steady stream of posts, and the accounts that stay consistent are the ones that break out. You won’t be making anything from scratch. Every clip and template is provided, so the work is in the assembly: pairing the right clip with the right hook, keeping it clean, and posting daily. The tutorial walks you through the exact process, and the sections below cover the rules that keep your posts approved and paid.
2. Content Format Requirements
Every clip must follow this workflow:
1. Pick a clip from the clips folder.
2. Pick a ready-made template.
3. Add the clip to the template.
4. Export and post to Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube.
Key Requirements
Keep these in mind on every clip:
* Do not make your own hooks.
* Use the hooks provided and make sure they are used properly.
* Add the clip properly inside the blank space provided in the template.
