ABOUT THE SHOW
Lisa Bloopy runs a sock company and is drowning in manual billing. She gets sucked into an ad and lands in Bilicon Valley, where she has to beat five trials to win her time back. Along the way she meets Bilbo Baggins (the “Lost Bill”), Billy Wonka, and William Shakespeare, and Brian Baumgartner hands her “touchless invoice processing sabers” to defeat her inner workaholic. She finds out Bill Pay can automate the grind. The finale: time is the one currency you can’t get back.
