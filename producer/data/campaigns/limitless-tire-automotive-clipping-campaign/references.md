GTA Tires, Wheels, Lift Kits, Auto Repair & Financing

Limitless Tire is a Canadian-owned automotive specialist serving drivers across Brampton, Mississauga, North York, Pickering, Burlington, and the wider Greater Toronto Area, including Rexdale and surrounding west-GTA communities. Since 2011 we have helped thousands of customers with premium tires, custom wheels and rims, lift kits, and full auto repair at five convenient branches.

Whether you need winter tires before the first Ontario snowfall, a staggered wheel package for your sports car, a suspension lift for your truck, or brake and alignment service to keep your family safe, our certified technicians deliver out-the-door pricing with no surprise fees. We carry hundreds of tire and wheel brands, Michelin, Bridgestone, Continental, Ferrada, Vossen, Fuel, and many more, with mount, balance, and road-hazard options on qualifying sets.

Need flexibility? Our no credit check financing program helps you get the tires, wheels, or complete package you need today with transparent payments from 1.5% per month, including $0 down and 90 days no payments on approved work. Frequently Asked Questions
Do you offer no credit check financing?

Yes. Limitless Tire offers transparent financing from 1.5% per month with no credit check required on qualifying tire and wheel packages. Apply online or in-store at any of our five GTA locations.

What tire brands do you carry?

We carry hundreds of premium tire brands including Michelin, Bridgestone, Continental, Pirelli, Goodyear, BFGoodrich, Falken, Toyo, Nitto, Hankook, and many more, with expert installation at all five GTA branches.

Do you install customer supplied tires?

Yes. We install customer-supplied tires and wheels when they meet safety and fitment requirements. Contact your nearest Limitless Tire location to confirm sizing, load rating, and appointment availability.

How much do lift kits cost?

Lift kit pricing depends on your vehicle, kit brand, and installation complexity. We provide out-the-door quotes after a fitment review. Financing is available at Brampton, Mississauga, North York, Pickering, and Burlington.

Do you offer wheel alignments?

Yes. Precision wheel alignment is available at every Limitless Tire location. Alignments help prevent uneven tire wear, improve handling, and extend tire life, especially after new tires, wheels, or suspension work.
