﻿ARON ORLANDO — “GO, FLY”
THE CAMPAIGN
Aron Orlando is introducing his upcoming 3-song project go, fly through a pre-release clipping campaign.
The goal is to make people feel like they’ve discovered an artist right before he blows up.
We aren't just trying to make the song go viral — we want people to discover Aron Orlando, remember his name, and become interested in the artist and the project.
Primary song: “go, fly”
Secondary: “watch me throw it away”
Release: October 16, 2026
Platforms: TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts
THE VISION
The content should feel:
* Cinematic
* Emotional
* Relatable
* Mysterious
* Aspirational
* Organic
Aron Orlando should feel like an artist people are discovering before everyone else catches on.
Think:
“Who is Aron Orlando?”
“How have I not heard of this guy?”
“I just found this artist and he’s about to be huge.”
The content should make the artist the discovery, not just the audio.
CONTENT
Aron will provide cinematic teaser videos featuring himself, voiceovers, handwritten text, and the songs.
These should be the main source material for the campaign.
Creators have a lot of freedom to:
* Re-edit the teasers
* Create their own concepts
* Use Aron’s footage
* Combine Aron’s footage with their own
* Use different parts of the songs
* Create their own hooks and storytelling
The strongest content should make viewers curious about Aron Orlando as an artist.
HOOKS
There is no required hook formula. Be creative and find what stops people from scrolling.
Hooks should ideally create artist discovery, rather than feeling like generic meme/POV content.
Examples of the direction:
* Make viewers feel like they just stumbled across an artist before he blows up.
* Introduce Aron in a way that makes people curious about who he is.
* Build intrigue around the fact that an unknown artist is making music at this level.
* Use Aron himself as the visual hook whenever possible.
* Create the feeling that viewers are discovering something they weren't supposed to find yet.
The goal: make people want to know who Aron Orlando is.
AUDIO
The campaign begins before the songs are released, so Aron will provide the audio.
Voiceovers can play before the song enters. The song should become the main focus once it hits.
Creators can use whatever section of the song works best. Aron will also provide recommended sections and blank templates.
After release, use the official platform audio where available.
RULES
Do:
* Keep content organic
* Experiment heavily
* Reuse concepts that perform
* Post across TikTok, Reels, and Shorts
* Keep successful posts live
* Let the music and artist speak for themselves
Don't:
* Buy views, likes, or followers
* Use bots or artificial engagement
* Use paid boosts
* Make the content feel like an advertisement
* Use AI-generated visuals
* Use NSFW, drugs, hate speech, politics, or other brand-damaging content
* Use unauthorized/stolen/private material
Third-party footage is allowed only when its use complies with applicable copyright and platform rules.
PAYMENT
$1.50 CPM
* Minimum qualifying views: 1,000
* Maximum payout per clip: $100
* Total campaign budget: $1,000
* Organic views only
No minimum follower count is required.
THE MAIN IDEA
Don't just make a viral clip with the song.
Make someone discover Aron Orlando.
Make them remember the name.
Make them feel like they found him before everyone else did.
