Influence AI — Campaign Brief
Everything you need to clip for this campaign. Read the whole doc before posting.


1. Guidelines & Requirements
* Clippers must have a dedicated page on TikTok just for Influence AI.
* Slideshows can't make Influence AI look bad (captions, editing, title).
* Your page must tag Influence AI in the bio of your account on the corresponding page.
* You must always tag Influence AI on the corresponding page on every slideshow.
* Engagement can't be botted in any way.
* Slideshows can only be posted on TikTok.
* Slideshows must target English-speaking countries only (USA, CA, AU, UK, NZ) with at least 50% of your audience from these regions.
* Payouts start every hour once your slideshow hits MVR & gets approved, so check the Content Rewards channel.
* Every slideshow posted must include the following in the description: “I’m using the @learn.influence (it will pop up as Influence AI) app.”
* You must always have a call to action (eg. app link in bio).
* AI clips are not allowed. Phonk music is not allowed.
* Bio must read: “Search ‘Influence AI’ - available on the App Store & Play Store.”
* The app screen must appear in the first half of every slideshow using the Influence AI app screen. The name shown on the app screen must be a girl’s name (eg. Mia, Emily), never the name of your account.
* The CTA slide must be the last slide of every slideshow.
* Use a dark, moody aesthetic throughout.
* Slideshows must be dark psychology content only, not rizz/pick-up-line content.
* Conversations must have a manipulative “evil-genius-master-psychologist” vibe.
* The tone must be sharp, unbothered, and calm. The character is always in control, never angry.
* Hook must contain phrasing like “Manipulating my GF” or “She regretted leaving.” Reference the provided examples for hook style.
Payouts are subject to available campaign funds at the time of approval. If the budget has been exhausted before your submission is reviewed, compensation cannot be guaranteed.

1. Campaign Overview
Influence AI is a psychology-based relationship advice app that helps users understand attraction, communication, and dating dynamics. This campaign is focused on driving downloads through TikTok slideshows only. Create engaging slideshows built on dark psychology and dark-triad themes, framed around recognizing and decoding manipulation: exposing the tactics manipulators use, breaking down manipulative conversations line by line, and teaching viewers how to spot those patterns. The viewer should feel like they’re gaining insider knowledge that most people don’t have. Strong first slide, clear CTA pointing to download Influence AI, and content that feels native to TikTok, not like an ad.
