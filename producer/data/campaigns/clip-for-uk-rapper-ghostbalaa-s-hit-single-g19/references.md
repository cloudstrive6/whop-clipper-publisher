3. Budget & Payout Structure
Total Campaign Budget: $1000
Preferred CPM: $1 per 1,000 views
Minimum payout per video: $10
Maximum payout per video: $150
Minimum like-to-view ratio: 1%
Example: A total budget of $1,000 with a $1 CPM will generate a minimum of 1,000,000 views. A $10 minimum payout incentivises creators to reach at least 10,000 views, while a $150 maximum payout encourages multiple high-performing uploads (150,000+ views). The minimum like-to-view ratio helps filter out low-effort or spam content.

5. Creative Brief
What to Do
This can be as specific or open as needed depending on the song, audience, and campaign goals.
Content types:
Content Guidance
* Money & Hustle - Content around making money, ambition, success, spending, lifestyle or getting to the bag.
* Talk vs Action - Play with the idea of people talking big but not backing it up. This can be humorous, relatable or serious.
* Lifestyle & Flex - Fashion, cars, nights out, jewellery, food, experiences or anything that naturally represents your lifestyle.
* POV / Relatable Content - Use the lyrics to soundtrack situations your audience would recognise or relate to.
* Performance / Lip-Sync - Put your own personality into performing or reacting to the lyrics rather than simply miming the track.
DOs
* Use open captions that remain positive, linking the edit to the song
* Tap into conversation around the song, visuals, or artist
* Commentary, reactions, or discussion are encouraged
* Optional on-screen text and captions should add context or meaning
* Avoid generic captions such as “this song is fire”
NOT ALLOWED
* Random or unrelated content that does not connect to the song or artist
* Low-effort, low-quality, or spam-style posts
* Misleading, negative, ironic, or controversial framing
* NSFW, violent, or sensitive subject matter
* Use of unapproved audio, footage, or alternate versions of the song
* Failure to meet required tagging, audio usage, or post duration rules
A clear “Not Allowed” section reduces rejections, saves approval time, prevents budget leakage, and sets a quality standard without micromanaging creativity.
