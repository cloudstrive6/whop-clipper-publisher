How the Game Works
Roll Anime Girls is an RNG tycoon on Roblox. You roll dice to unlock anime girls, then place them on your plot where they generate money, including while you're offline.
Characters can follow you around, and you can buy potions to boost your luck for rarer pulls. Once you've built up enough, you rebirth to reset progress in exchange for permanent boosts, then climb again faster.
