Fold  Campaign Rules
 Campaign Info
Fold is a Bitcoin rewards company focused on making Bitcoin part of everyday life.
The campaign is focused on general Fold awareness, everyday spending, bitcoin rewards, debit card usage, and merchant gift cards ahead of the Reserve launch.
Content should feel native to social media, entertaining before promotional, simple, relatable, and creator-led.
 Requirements
RULES
Platforms: TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, and X
Every clip must clearly connect back to Fold
Keep Fold as the clear subject of the content
Use the latest approved Fold disclosure guidance
Required paid disclosure: "Sponsored by Fold. I was compensated to share this."
Make the disclosure visible and readable
Use approved Fold footage/assets as the base when clipping source material
Keep all important product context and qualifiers intact
Do not make unsupported claims about Fold or its products
Reserve content is separate and must follow the separate approved Reserve brief and disclosure rules
HIGH PRIORITY FORMAT
Hook in the first 1–3 seconds
Trending, native pacing and native audio when appropriate
Open on a relatable everyday moment, strong reaction, question, or interesting statement
Make the content entertaining before promotional
The viewer should understand the Fold connection quickly
Let the creator's personality carry the content
Do not make clips feel like traditional finance advertisements
OVERALL CONTENT STYLE
Real, native creator content
POV and first-person angles
Meme and comedy formats when they fit
Everyday spending and lifestyle content
Simple Bitcoin education
Personal finance conversations
Bitcoin-native commentary for crypto audiences
Strong storytelling
Authentic opinions and reactions
Quality over quantity
CONTENT ANGLES
POINTS VS BITCOIN
Compare the experience of traditional rewards with Bitcoin rewards
POVs, memes, reactions, and commentary are encouraged
Keep comparisons general
Do not directly attack specific competitors
Do not make claims about future Bitcoin value
Don't say or imply that Bitcoin rewards are worth more than points or cash back, or that they will grow in value
EVERYDAY SPENDING
Show Fold fitting naturally into purchases people already make
Coffee, food, shopping, gas, and everyday expenses
Show how Fold can make normal spending more rewarding
Keep the value simple and easy to understand
BITCOIN MADE SIMPLE
Make Bitcoin approachable for people who are curious but not crypto experts
Keep explanations simple
Avoid technical explanations
Focus on making Bitcoin feel accessible and part of everyday life
BITCOIN / STACKING SATS
Bitcoin creators
Crypto creators
Finance creators
Bitcoin communities on X
More Bitcoin-native language is appropriate for audiences that already understand it
Content must still clearly connect back to Fold
MERCHANT GIFT CARDS
Merchant gift cards are an approved Fold content angle
Show the everyday use case and Bitcoin-back rewards
Keep all product claims consistent with approved Fold materials
STRICTLY NOT ALLOWED
Do not post clips containing:
Guaranteed earnings claims
Risk-free claims
Investment-return promises
Bitcoin price predictions
Claims that users will become wealthy
Unsupported personal investment claims
Unsupported product claims
Misleading captions
Misleading edits
Content that removes important context from a statement
Generic Bitcoin/crypto content with no meaningful Fold connection
Any mention of Fold Reserve, including Reserve rates, tiers, bonuses, or member benefits
Anything that could misrepresent Fold or its products
Saying “every purchase” or “every swipe” earns bitcoin. Use “eligible purchases.”
Fee claims such as “free,” “no fees,” “zero fees,” or “no spread”
Calling Fold a bank, describing rewards as interest, yield, or savings, or saying rewards or bitcoin are FDIC insured
Fold+
Fold Holdings stock ($FLD)
Do not post content simply because it may generate engagement if it violates these rules.
IMPORTANT
Non-compliant content does not qualify for payout. Fold may require you to edit or remove any post at any time, including after payout.
DISCLOSURES
Every paid post must use the current approved Fold disclosure.
Required caption:
Generally: "Ad. Sponsored by Fold. I was compensated to share this."
Any post that mentions rewards, bitcoin back, the debit card, or gift cards, add:
"Fold, Inc. is not a bank. The Fold Prepaid Debit Card is issued by Sutton Bank, Member FDIC, pursuant to a license from Visa U.S.A. Inc. Rewards are provided by Fold, not by Sutton Bank. They are unsecured promotional obligations of Fold, Inc., are not deposits, are not FDIC insured, and may lose value. Gift cards are issued by each brand and subject to its terms. Terms apply."
Follow the latest Fold-approved placement requirements for the platform being used.
CONTENT QUALITY
The main goal is to make Fold feel native to the feed.
Keep the creator front and center
Prioritize strong hooks and entertaining moments
Make the Fold connection obvious
Use your own editing style and knowledge of the audience
Do not make the content feel like a corporate ad
Do not over-script the content
Do not turn the clip into a complicated financial explanation
Captions are encouraged when they improve context, retention, engagement, or understanding
Captions must accurately reflect the video
