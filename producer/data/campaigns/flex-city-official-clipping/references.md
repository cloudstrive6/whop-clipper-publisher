 4. Ideas for cutting content
 Drift + Music
Aggressive phonk / hard beats, drift synced to music drops, speed ramps + motion blur + camera shakes. Night driving performs best ​
Examples:
https://www.tiktok.com/@azzmedft2/video/7605160184918379794
https://www.tiktok.com/@azzmedft2/video/7598166286761348360
 Tap Interaction Format
Viral interaction format where on-screen prompts appear 1–2 seconds before the action.
Examples: “DOUBLE TAP TO START THE ENGINE”, “TAP TO ESCAPE THE POLICE”.
Examples: https://www.tiktok.com/@csrracing/video/7618269906760469782
 AI / Split-Screen Reviewer
Top half = gameplay, bottom half = reaction / AI commentary.
Hook examples: “This mobile driving game feels illegal…”, “Why is nobody talking about Flex City?”, “I didn’t expect mobile graphics to look like this”.
Examples:

Progression format: beginner character with cheap cars → supercars, penthouse, money flex, gang/police status.

Story-style POV content.
Examples: “POV: first day in Flex City”, “POV: you joined the wrong gang”, “POV: police caught you drifting”.
