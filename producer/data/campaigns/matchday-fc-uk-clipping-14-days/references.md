Your club. Your crest. Your squad. Open packs, pull real stars, upgrade your favorites, rise up the leagues. Football. Cards. Chaos.

Rip the pack. There's your first star.

This is soccer card collecting at its purest. Open packs, pull real players, and build the squad you've always wanted, one card at a time.

What you'll do:

Open packs that drop with celebration. Every pull lands with weight, the big names, the rising stars, the players you'll be glad you grabbed early. The reveal is the reward.

Build your starting XI from the cards you pull. Pick your favorites, shape your lineup, make it unmistakably yours. Your squad, your call.

Upgrade your players and climb the leagues match by match. Coins and Gems power the climb. Stamina paces it. The progression is honest, and your squad earns its way up.

Discover stars from leagues around the world. Real players, real names, real moments, pulled into a squad only you could build.

New in Matchday, two new ways to grow your Fans and build your club:

Locker Raid. Get sent to a rival's stadium, open their lockers, and haul off the Fans they left loose. Spend your own Fans on your stadium to keep them safe.

Fan Takeover. Send your traveling support to take over a rival's stadium and watch your Fans pour in, your colors flying until the rival reclaims it. Hold Shields to defend your own.

And two more ways to play:

Friends & Feed. Add friends by code or invite, climb the Friends Leaderboard against your friends and the world, and follow your circle and your rivalries in the Timeline.

Team of the Season. Pull the season's standout players in a new limited-time edition, from packs built for the drop.

Take it to the pitch:

Matches play out in fast highlights, and the biggest moments are yours to play. Time the shot, win the duel, finish in stoppage time. There's always something new in season too, live events, weekly competitions, and fresh mini-games to play.

Why fans keep their squad close:

Matchday fits the spare minutes. Halftime, the commute, the buzz before kickoff. Short sessions, real reward moments, and a squad that keeps growing whether you have two minutes or twenty.

The cards are real players. The packs feel like opening day. The squad feels like yours.

The leagues are calling. Open your first pack and see who you'll pull.

Before You Spend Money, Read This
Jun 5
ChaosM94

I am reviewing this game based on my experience with the previous version released by the same developers.The original game was genuinely fun and had a player-driven trading market where players could buy and sell cards with each other. I invested thousands of dollars into that game and became the #1 player with one of the strongest collections in the community.Then the game was shut down. We were initially told an update was coming, but the game never returned in its original form. For about a year, many players in our Discord community repeatedly asked for updates and received little to no communication. In the end, all of our collections, cards, progress, and investments were gone.Now a new version has been released, but based on my first day playing, progression appears heavily limited by stamina. Players recover only a small amount of stamina over long periods of time, or they can spend money to continue playing. Card packs are purchased with premium currency, and from day one there were paid offers costing around $100 to guarantee a promotional card.My advice is simple: enjoy the game if you want, but think carefully before spending money. I trusted the previous game with a significant investment, and that investment disappeared when the original game was shut down.Take this review however you want, but it comes from someone who was the #1 player in the previous game.
