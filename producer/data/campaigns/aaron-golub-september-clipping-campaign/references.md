﻿Aaron Golub — Leadership, Adversity & Authority Clips
✔ You do not need a dedicated Aaron Golub page to participate. You may post from an existing motivational, leadership, sports, personal development, or business content page.
CAMPAIGN GOAL
The goal of this campaign is to expand the reach of Aaron Golub’s personal brand, introduce his ideas to new audiences, and drive qualified viewers to his social profiles. The higher-value outcome is to generate interest in Aaron Golub as a keynote speaker and encourage companies, conferences, sports teams, schools, and other organisations to consider booking him to speak.
Clips should position Aaron Golub as a compelling authority on:
* Overcoming adversity and converting failures into lessons and opportunities
* Leadership — leading with influence, not titles
* Shattering limiting beliefs and rethinking your “impossible”
* Thinking differently about disabilities and creating environments where everyone belongs
* Grit, determination, and effort as the foundations of success
* Asking for help as a strength — asking instead of assuming
* Preparation and mindset for high performance
Content should feel native to social media — not like a branded advertisement.
CAPTION REQUIREMENT
Every caption must include:
* A short takeaway, opinion, or question related to the specific clip.
* The client’s official tagged account.
* A clear call to action encouraging viewers to consider Aaron Golub for a speaking opportunity.
Required Call to Action
End every caption with a clear, natural CTA that positions Aaron Golub as a keynote speaker and encourages the viewer to book, invite, or consider him for their next event, team, organisation, or audience.
The CTA should be adapted to the subject of the clip where possible, but the intent should remain clear: Aaron is available as a keynote speaker and the viewer should consider bringing him in to speak.
Examples:
* Book Aaron Golub as a keynote speaker for your next event. Learn more at @aaronjgolub*.*
* Looking for a keynote speaker who knows what it means to overcome the impossible? Book Aaron at @aaronjgolub*.*
* Want Aaron to bring this message to your team? Book him to speak at @aaronjgolub*.*
* Bring Aaron’s story of overcoming adversity to your next event. Learn more about booking him at @aaronjgolub*.*
* Looking for a speaker for your next leadership event? Consider Aaron Golub. Learn more at @aaronjgolub*.*
* Want Aaron to speak to your organisation, team, or audience? Connect with him at @aaronjgolub*.*
* Bring Aaron Golub to your next conference, team, or event. Learn more at @aaronjgolub*.*
These are examples of the style we want, not lines to copy word for word. Write the CTA in your own words where a more natural phrasing fits the clip.
The CTA should connect naturally to the content. For example:
* Aaron’s football story: Encourage viewers to bring his story to their team, school, or audience.
* Leadership content: Encourage companies and leadership teams to consider Aaron for a leadership event or speaking engagement.
* Overcoming adversity: Encourage audiences looking for a motivational or keynote speaker to consider Aaron.
* Disability and belonging: Encourage organisations interested in inclusion, belonging, and leadership to consider Aaron as a speaker.
Do not use generic “follow for more” CTAs as the required call to action. The primary CTA should be about booking or inviting Aaron to speak.
Do not use overly aggressive sales language, fabricated urgency, rates, availability claims, “DM to book,” or invented booking links.
Recommended Caption Format

Caption Guidelines
✔ Keep the opening line relevant to the specific clip
✔ Keep captions concise and conversational
✔ Tag the correct client account
✔ End with a clear speaking CTA
✔ Use a CTA that fits the subject and audience of the clip
✔ Use two to four relevant hashtags when appropriate
❌ Do not use unrelated viral captions
❌ Do not make claims the client does not make in the video
❌ Do not use excessive or irrelevant hashtags
❌ Do not write captions as though the client personally posted the video
❌ Do not use first-person language such as “I believe” when referring to the client
❌ Do not use fabricated rates, availability, urgency, or booking information
❌ Do not use “DM to book” or invented booking links
IMPORTANT CREATIVE DIRECTION
The best-performing clips will not feel like advertisements for the client. They should make the viewer feel like they have discovered:
* A powerful idea
* A new way to think about themselves or their business
* A contrarian perspective
* A practical lesson
* A speaker or expert they want to hear more from
The objective is to make viewers stop scrolling, watch the full clip, become interested in Aaron Golub and his perspective, and recognise him as a speaker they could bring to their company, team, school, conference, or event.
The content should earn the speaking CTA rather than feeling like a speaking advertisement. The viewer should first find Aaron’s story, ideas, or perspective compelling, then be given a clear next step: book or invite Aaron to speak.
Profile visits can support this journey, but the primary conversion objective is generating interest in Aaron Golub as a keynote speaker.
