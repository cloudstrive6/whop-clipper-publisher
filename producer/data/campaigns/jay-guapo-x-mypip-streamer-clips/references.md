The 7 rules, and how to actually pass them
Every clip has to pass all 7. Miss one and the clip pays nothing. No partial credit.
Rule 1: At least 40% of viewers from the USA and Canada
Combined, not each. 33% USA + 8% Canada = 41%, that passes. UK and everywhere else do not count toward this one.
How to pass it:
Post between 12pm and 10pm Eastern. That is when North America is awake and scrolling.
Hook text and captions in English only.
Lean into American streamer culture. Kai Cenat, Reggie, Twitch and Kick references are your best friend here, that audience is almost entirely US.
During warm up, follow and engage with US streamer and clip accounts. Your first viewers look like the accounts you interact with.
Set your account to your real region and do not use a VPN.
Never buy views. Bought views come from wherever is cheapest and will tank this number instantly.
Rule 2: At least 2,000 views
How to pass it:
Warm the page up before posting. A cold account gets capped at a couple hundred views.
The first half second decides everything. Open on the funniest frame, not on Jay typing.
Post at consistent times.
If a clip dies, do not delete it. Post the next one.
Rule 3: No AI, anywhere in the clip
Not allowed: Opus Clip or any auto clipping tool, AI avatars, AI voiceover or text to speech, AI generated b roll, AI upscaling, AI extended or altered footage, AI faceless narration.
Allowed: auto transcription to generate your subtitles, as long as you fix every mistake. That is a tool for typing, not for making content.
How to pass it: cut it yourself, from the real footage. That is it.
Rule 4: Only the provided video
Every frame of the clip comes from the video we gave you. The main footage, the audio, and the moment itself all come from the provided video.
Rule 5: MyPip has to be in the clip
One of these has to be true:
The MyPip screen is visible and Jay is using it (typing a prompt, watching it build, playing the game it made, editing it), or
Jay is talking about MyPip (the app, the game he made, launching it, what he thinks of it)
If neither is true, it is just a Jay Guapo clip and it does not get paid.
Rule 6: Full analytics screen recording
A screen recording, not screenshots, showing every tab and every stat for that clip. Taken after the clip has passed 2,000 views. Full breakdown is in How to submit and get paid.
Rule 7: At least 1% engagement rate
Engagement = (likes + comments) ÷ views. Only likes and comments count. Shares, saves and follows do not.
On a 10,000 view clip you need 100 likes and comments combined. On 100,000 views you need 1,000.
How to pass it:
Ask a question in your caption. "would you play this?" gets comments. "crazy clip" gets nothing.
Pin a comment with an opinion the second you post. Give people something to reply to.
Reply to every comment in the first hour. Replies count as comments and they bring more.
Clip moments people have opinions on. The Reggie and Kai moments and the "is this game fire or trash" moments get people typing.
Never buy likes or comments. Bought engagement gets the clip rejected and you removed.
