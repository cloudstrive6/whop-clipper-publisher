﻿McQueen Clipping Requirements
Budget: $5,000 Rate: $0.75 / 1,000 views Max Per Clip: $200 Platforms: TikTok,Instagram Reels Clip Length: 20–60 seconds
Goal
Drive real viewers to mcqueennlive on Twitch. Every clip should make someone stop scrolling and want to watch him live. Make people feel something.
Account Requirements
You must have one of the following to participate:
* A dedicated account posting exclusively McQueen content
* A themed streamer/gaming page with 5,000+ followers
Source Material
All clips must come from VODs provided by admins only. Do not clip from the Twitch clip section.
What to Clip
The focus is comedy. If it made you laugh or made chat explode, clip it.
* Funny public interactions or reactions
* Unexpected or chaotic moments
* Strong reactions — shock, excitement, disbelief
* Moments that made chat go crazy
* Anything that makes someone want to watch more
Do Not Clip
* Content from the Twitch clip section
* McQueen talking about "pimping" or anything from his old brand
* Dead air or slow moments
* Poor audio or video quality
* Clips under 20 or over 60 seconds
Violating the old branding rule repeatedly will result in removal from the campaign.
Funneling — How to Convert Viewers
Getting views is only half the job. Every clip should actively push people to Twitch.
On-Screen CTA (Required)
Every clip must include a text overlay CTA. Most viewers never read the description. Put it on screen.
Link in Bio (Required)
Your link in bio must point directly to mcqueennlive on Twitch at all times. On TikTok, the bio link is the only tappable option this is non-negotiable. 
Engage in Your Comments (Required)
When someone comments asking "who is this?" or shows interest, reply with the Twitch link. Every curious comment is a warm lead. Do not leave them unanswered.
CTAs — Make Them Feel Urgent
Generic CTAs get ignored. Lead with curiosity and urgency, not just location. Approved CTAs:
* If you need something to watch this week tune in with mcqueennlive on Twitch
* If you’re not watching mcqueen now is the time tune into mcqueennlive on Twitch
* Watch the full live at mcqueennlive on Twitch
* Catch McQueen live at mcqueennlive on Twitch
* Fire streams daily at mcqueennlive on Twitch
Pin your CTA as the top comment on every post.
Story Posts (Recommended)
We strongly recommend posting a story on TikTok or Instagram every 1–2 days. Stories hit your warmest audience people who already follow you. Use them to push McQueen's stream while he is actually live for the best results.
Each story should include a clip and a visible CTA directing viewers to Twitch. Send a screenshot to an admin after posting.
* Link in bio to watch McQueen live every day
* Click here to watch McQueen's fire streams
* He's live right now at Twitch: mcqueennlive
Campaign Standards
* Top 3 audience countries must include the US, UK, and/or Canada
* Every clip must have a title
* No botting or fake engagement
* High-quality edits only
* Do not make McQueen look bad or take clips out of context
* Clips must reach 5,000 views to be eligible for payout
Failure to follow these guidelines may result in rejected clips or removal from the campaign.
