Getting started
Post ready-made Wiped clips or create your own UGC (user-generated content) for TikTok or Instagram Reels and earn on verified views. Choose the style you prefer: simple app clips, demos, or creative promotional videos. Wiped lets iPhone users change their GPS location and simulate routes.

Download ready-made videos, request custom media, or request a Wiped media account. For quick help with content requests, media accounts, or setup, message us on Instagram. You can also open a support ticket at wiped.me/feedback 
1. Set up your account
Use a public, active TikTok or Instagram account that can receive normal organic engagement. You can create a dedicated account or use your existing creator account for UGC.
Choose any username or display name you like. You do not need to include Wiped or usewiped.com in your name.
Put usewiped.com in your bio before posting. Example: Looking for the app? usewiped.com. If you make UGC, you can clearly show usewiped.com as readable on-screen text in the video instead. Keep your chosen placement visible while views are tracked.
Download the Wiped profile photo here to use on a dedicated account. If you make UGC on your own creator account, you can keep your existing profile photo. Check that usewiped.com is in your bio or clearly shown in your UGC video before posting.
2. Look at these examples
TikTok: @usewiped and @usewiped.com
Look at their bios, profile setup, video types, and captions for ideas. Use them as inspiration; do not download or submit another creator's posts.

Custom media

Create your own UGC
Request a Wiped media account on the clipping page. We are happy to provide an account and help you access and set up the app. Create your own screen recordings, tutorials, voiceovers, on-camera videos, or other original content promoting Wiped. Choose whichever format suits you, or mix styles.
Posting rules
Follow these rules on every post. Only public posts with genuine, eligible views can be approved.
Use the right content and format
Post a public TikTok video or Instagram Reel from your account. Use ready-made clips, custom Wiped media, your own app recordings, or original UGC. All of these options are welcome.
You can edit clips and add captions, voiceovers, or your own creative style. Keep the promotion accurate and, when showing the app, keep Wiped branding, the app interface, and important on-screen text clear and readable.
Do not add another brand's watermark, logo, end card, or promotional message. Do not take content from another creator or page.
Write a clear caption
Keep the caption short, accurate, and relevant to the video. Check spelling and formatting. Do not claim Wiped is 'guaranteed undetectable' or imply an endorsement Wiped has not approved.
Include any sponsorship or advertising disclosure required by your platform or local law. Include usewiped.com in your bio or clearly show it in your UGC video; wiped.me/feedback.  is available for support.
Keep posts public for at least 15 days
Keep each post public for at least 15 days after posting and while views are being tracked. Deleting, archiving, making the account private, or removing the required website placement can lead to rejection or loss of eligibility.
Private accounts, close-friends posts, stories, and broken links cannot be verified.
Use organic engagement only
Views, likes, comments, shares, and follows must come from genuine users. Do not buy views or use bots, view farms, engagement groups, traffic exchanges, or paid boosting.
Do not ask people to repeatedly refresh or loop a post to inflate views. Suspicious or artificial traffic may lead to rejection and non-payment.
Avoid duplicate posts and submissions
Do not upload the same clip more than once to the same account. Submit each live post URL only once.
Common rejection reasons
Unrelated content or another creator's reposted content; duplicate submissions; artificial traffic; private, deleted, archived, or story-only posts; broken links; missing usewiped.com from the bio (or from the video for UGC); misleading claims; added branding; or an invalid format.
Payouts
$2 USD per 1,000 valid views
Minimum payout: $2 per post, reached at 1,000 valid views.
Maximum payout: $150 per approved post.
Payout examples
1,000 views: $2   |   10,000: $20   |   25,000: $50
50,000 views: $100   |   100,000: $150 (capped)
Total campaign budget: $1,500 USD, shared across creators. The campaign may close when the available budget is used.
Submit & checklist
Submit your post
1. Choose your preferred style: ready-made clips, custom media, your own app recordings, or original UGC.
2. Post it as a public TikTok video or Instagram Reel. Check that usewiped.com is in your bio or clearly shown in your UGC video, and follow the Posting Rules section.
3. Copy the live post URL and submit it through the campaign dashboard. Submit each URL only once.
4. Keep the post public for at least 15 days and while views are tracked. Follow approval and payout status in the dashboard.
Wiped reviews the live post and checks the rules. Only verified, eligible views count toward your final payout.
Final checklist
[ ] Account and post are public; any username or display name is welcome.
[ ] usewiped.com is in your bio or clearly shown in your UGC video.
[ ] Content uses ready-made clips, custom media, your own app recordings, or original UGC.
[ ] Content promotes Wiped accurately and follows the posting rules.
[ ] The caption is accurate and includes any required disclosure.
[ ] No paid, botted, boosted, or manipulated engagement was used.
[ ] The live URL is correct and has not already been submitted.
[ ] The post will stay public for at least 15 days and during tracking.
