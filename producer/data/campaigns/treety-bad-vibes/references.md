﻿Treety - Bad Vibes 
Clipping Campaign Guidelines & Posting Requirements

Campaign Overview:
This campaign is focused on pushing Treety’s Bad Vibes through simple, highly shareable TikTok-style content built around the official audio.
The song is upbeat, bouncy, and already has enough energy to carry simple edits. Do not overthink the creative. The goal is to make posts that feel native to TikTok and look like something someone would naturally post because they liked the song, not like an advertisement for Treety.
	Audio Link: https://www.tiktok.com/music/Bad-Vibes-7688153617895786513

Approved Creative Angles
  CREATIVE 1: RELATABLE TOS + FUNNY / DANCE FOOTAGE
Concept
	Pair funny, energetic, awkward, chaotic, or entertaining footage with short relatable text on screen.
The footage does not need to literally match the text. In many cases, the humor comes from pairing a relatable situation with ridiculous or overly energetic footage. 
	Source
	* People turning up
* Awkward dancing
* Old viral clips
* People dramatically walking away
* Funny reactions
* People celebrating
* Groups dancing
* Someone moving way too hard to the music
* Random clips that visually match the energy of the song
	TOS Examples
	“me after saying I’m staying in this weekend”
“when the plans finally leave the groupchat”
“me after getting the smallest amount of good news”
“me after checking my account and realizing I can afford ONE unnecessary purchase”
“how I walk away after starting an argument I know I’m wrong in”
“me acting like my life isn’t falling apart because the song hard”
“POV: your friend says ‘we’re just getting food’ and pulls up like this”
“me when I finally get paid after checking my account every 30 minutes”
“when you said you weren’t going out but they send you the address”
“me after doing one productive thing all day”
	Example Posts: 
	https://www.tiktok.com/t/ZP8TmCgy8/
	

 CREATIVE 2: FEMALE RELATIONSHIP / “MY MAN BE ON BS” CONTENT

Concept 
	Create relatable relationship content designed primarily for a female audience.
These should feel like the type of posts people send to their friends, repost, quote, or argue about in the comments.
The humor can be messy, dramatic, delusional, jealous, stubborn, or slightly toxic, but it should still feel playful and relatable rather than hateful or extreme.
	Source Footage 
	* Women walking
* Getting ready
* Mirror clips
* Sitting in a car
* Looking at a phone
* Dark-room footage
* Dancing
* Girls hanging out together
* Reaction clips
* Relationship scenes
* Someone walking away dramatically
* Fashion / lifestyle footage
* B-roll where the subject’s face is not clearly visible
	TOS Ideas
	“me going back to fix things with my man because nobody else is getting him”
“when he thinks we broke up but I just needed 4 hours to calm down”
“me after blocking him knowing damn well I’m checking if he called”
“when my man starts acting single so now we both single”
“I don’t want a new man I want MY man to act right”
“me pulling up to address something I said I was over 3 days ago”
“when he finally apologizes but I already told the groupchat everything”
“me when another girl thinks she discovered MY problem”
“he can leave me alone but not TOO alone”
“when I say I’m done but everybody knows I’m lying”
“me saying I’m never talking to him again knowing I’ll probably answer tonight”
“when your friends hate him but unfortunately that is still your man”
	Example Post: 
	https://www.tiktok.com/t/ZP8TmqfPt/
	

 CREATIVE 3: MEME FORMATS / “HOW WE MOVING TO THIS SONG” CREATIVE 3: MEME FORMATS
Concept
	Use funny dance clips, reaction clips, viral footage, recognizable meme formats, or current events people are actively talking about online and build the post around the energy of the song.
You are encouraged to turn current viral moments, celebrity news, sports moments, internet drama, or other non-serious events people are already talking about into memes using the song.
For example, Taylor Swift recently went viral for pointing to her ring and yelling “That’s my husband” after Travis Kelce scored a touchdown. The internet immediately started using the moment to joke about women hyping their man up for doing the bare minimum
Another current example is MrBeast and Erling Haaland recreating the viral AI flinch meme, which has been circulating heavily online this week. That could become something as simple as:
* “me trying not to react when my friend says she’s going back to him again”
* “me pretending I didn’t see his name pop up on her phone”
* “me trying to stay out of relationship drama that has absolutely nothing to do with me”
	Source
	Current viral content from TikTok, Instagram, X, YouTube, sports pages, entertainment pages, celebrity news, interviews, award shows, trending TV/movie moments, and other widely discussed internet moments.

	POSTS WILL BE REJECTED FOR
	Use the approved Treety ‘Bad Vibes’  audio
	Overly sexual content
	Keep the song clearly audible
	Offensive or brand-damaging content
	Follow one of the approved creative directions above
	Graphic violence or disturbing footage
	Use original edits
	Completely random footage with no clear concept
	Source your own footage / clips
	Another creator’s finished edit reposted with no meaningful changes
	Avoid reposting another editor’s finished content
	Content that does not fit any approved creative direction
	Avoid visible TikTok, Instagram, CapCut creator, or editor watermarks whenever possible
	Using the wrong audio
	Use clean, readable TOS
	Low-quality footage
	Make the concept understandable within the first few seconds
	Content clearly designed as an advertisement
	Stay public for a minimum of 30 days
	Posts that could reasonably create negative controversy around Treety rather than around the joke itself
