GET PAID

All payouts run through Whop's Content Rewards. Here's the flow from posting to cash-out.

OPEN THE CONTENT REWARDS APP
You'll find it inside the Whop Community. This is where you submit your clip links and track approval status.
VERIFY YOUR WHOP ACCOUNT
Before you can be paid, you need to verify your account by connecting your socials. Go to Whop → Settings → Connected Accounts.
SUBMIT YOUR VIDEO LINKS
Once verified, head back to Content Rewards and paste the link to the clip you've posted. Submit within 30 minutes of posting.
HIT MVR AND GET APPROVED
Once your video crosses the Minimum View Requirement and an approver signs off, your payout queues up automatically.

Questions? Open a support ticket in the Reach Discord. For Whop-specific issues such as payouts or account verification, contact Whop support.

To hit the audience requirement, make sure your Streamer content reads as US-targeted.

Use English-language captions and on-screen text.
Avoid country-specific slang from outside the target regions.
Tag locations as US cities when relevant.
If you have access to creator-side audience insights, check that your historical audience is majority US/UK/CA/AU/NZ before joining.
