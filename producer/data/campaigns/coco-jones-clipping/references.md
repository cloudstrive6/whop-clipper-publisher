THE MISSION
You’ve seen her before and clipped for her! CoCo Jones released some new footage to post on your pages, cut the empty space, make the clips engaging and tell a story.

	The Vibe
	

Every clip should feel raw and authentic, CoCo Jones talking about how she met her husband Donovan. Her doing a photoshoot and the raw “runway” walk down the street crossing. And their interview moment.

These are the clips, you build the story and an engaging caption using the footage. These are directions, not exact scripts.

* Coco Jones and her husband Donovan say who they could beat in tennis
