Abu Lahya Clipping Guidelines
ATTRACTION FIRST • TRUST SECOND • PROOF WHEN AVAILABLE
	

Who is Abu Lahya: Abu Lahya is a serial entrepreneur, content creator, and prominent figure in the online Muslim business space. He is best known for founding the IMA Accelerator, an educational mentorship platform tailored specifically for Muslim entrepreneurs.

01  WHAT CLIPPING IS MAINLY FOR
Priority 1 — Attraction
Pull in the right Muslim men who feel stuck, want more from life, want halal income, and want to improve themselves.
Priority 2 — Trust
Make Eyoub feel real, sincere, principled, morally safe, worth listening to, and endorsed by other trusted people.
Priority 3 — Light Proof
If a clip naturally includes a student win, result, transformation, or credibility moment, use it.
THE SIMPLE RULE
Clipping = attraction first, trust second, proof when available.
	

02  WHAT CLIPS WE WANT MOST
A. Attraction Clips
• A strong opinion
• A surprising claim
• A painful truth
• A challenge to lazy thinking
• A strong Islamic / masculine / ambitious identity angle
B. Trust Clips
These should make viewers feel that Eyoub is sincere, has standards, genuinely cares, lives what he says, and is serious, disciplined, and principled.
• His values and halal standards
• What he refuses to do
• Family, responsibility, parents, marriage, and deen
• Lessons from hardship
• Vulnerability or sacrifice
• Serious moral conviction
C. Light Proof Clips
• Student wins
• Transformations
• Testimonials
• Outcomes
• Stories of effort paying off
• Credibility moments that feel natural — never forced
03  HOOK STYLES THAT ALREADY WORK
1. Contrarian hook
Attacks a common belief.
“I made money having the worst routine.”
“You cannot just make dua about money and then refresh your PayPal.”
“I think you’re overhyping rich people.”
Why it works: Breaks expectations fast.
2. Identity / unfair advantage hook
Speaks directly to Muslims and gives them belief.
“Muslims don’t understand. We have like an unfair advantage.”
Why it works: Creates identity, pride, and curiosity.
3. Pain-based hook
Names a real painful problem.
“When people have an actual problem that’s painful, the only thing they want to do is get out of it.”
Why it works: Pain grabs attention faster than abstract advice.
4. Vulnerable / story hook
Opens with loss, struggle, or risk.
“I bought a big mentorship and I got scammed.”
“There’s no way I can even make a thousand bucks.”
Why it works: Feels human and creates instant tension.
5. Specific proof hook
Uses a sharp number, result, or timeline.
“$3,000 in six days from scratch.”
“Do you know that Alex Hormozi charges a million dollars for a day?”
Why it works: Specificity feels real and creates curiosity.
6. Duty / values hook
Ties money to responsibility, family, or deen.
“If your parents haven’t performed Umrah and Hajj yet, take that as a first responsibility.”
Why it works: Makes the message deeper than money.
7. Hardline conviction hook
Shows strong standards or refusal.
“I never negotiated the IMA price.”
Why it works: Signals authority and standards.
04  WHAT TO LOOK FOR WHEN SELECTING CLIPS
✓ Saying something bold
✓ Challenging a weak belief
✓ Calling men higher
✓ Tying money to deen, family, or duty
✓ Exposing a scam, mistake, or lesson
✓ Showing standards or principles
✓ Making Islam feel like strength, not limitation
✓ Making success feel honorable and halal
✓ Sounding witty, sharp, or unapologetic
✓ Giving a memorable line
A STRONG CLIP USUALLY HAS
A great first line • one clear idea • emotional charge • clean payoff • no long rambling before the point.
	

05  WHAT *NOT* TO CLIP
• Primarily other people speaking (back-and-forth is fine)
• Too long to get to the point
• Too dependent on context
• Repetitive with no strong takeaway
• Too soft, generic, or motivational
• Too academic
• Too scattered with multiple ideas
• Aggressive without value
• Controversial only for the sake of controversy (“Tate vibes”)
• Anything that gives generic guru vibes
Do not force a clip just because the guest is famous, the setting looks good, or it sounds smart but has no punch.
Example to avoid: next.frame.io/share/0a5953da-b51f-4921-a42a-eb3f1f003912/view/30cb7155-a7e9-4dfc-b654-7ffef5a133b8
06  HALAL / BRAND SAFETY RULES
NON-NEGOTIABLE
Every clip must protect the halal positioning and the seriousness of the brand.
	

No music
No background music, trending sounds, or subtle instrumental beds. Nasheed/halal audio with no instruments is accepted
No women / immodest visuals
• Women or female models
• Mixed random B-roll with women in frame
• Social media screenshots or clips showing women
• Any visual that weakens the halal positioning
THIS IS VERY IMPORTANT.
No haram imagery
• Nightlife or clubs
• Alcohol
• Gambling
• Revealing clothing
• Flashy flex edits with haram associations
Protect the message
• No meme edits that cheapen the message
• No deceptive editing that twists what Eyoub meant
• No rage-bait just for the sake of rage-bait
• No edits that make Eyoub feel arrogant, insincere, or unserious
The goal is sharp + principled — not ego for the sake of ego.
07  EDITING STYLE GUIDELINES
Keep It
✓ Clean
✓ Masculine
✓ Simple
✓ Premium
✓ Serious
✓ Easy to understand
Preferred Style
• Fast start
• Crisp captions
• Clean punch-ins
• Subtle movement
• Strong readability
• Minimal distractions
• Let the words do the work
Avoid
• Overediting
• Flashy transitions
• Too many sound effects
• Gimmicky zooms
• Noisy subtitles
• Childish meme style
• Random stock footage unless it genuinely helps
EY0UB SHOULD FEEL
Calm • sharp • convicted • mature • trustworthy • high standard.
	

08  LINK IN BIO — REQUIRED
PUT THE LINK IN YOUR BIO - https://go.imaccelerator.com/110
Every clipping account must have the campaign / Eyoub link placed clearly in the account bio. The goal is to turn attention from the clips into profile visits and clicks.
	

✓ Make sure the link is live and clickable before posting.
✓ Keep the bio clean so viewers immediately understand where to go next.
✓ Do not replace the required link with unrelated promotions or offers.
✓ Check the link regularly and fix it immediately if it stops working.
09  SIMPLE CLIP FILTER BEFORE EXPORTING
1. Does the first line stop the scroll?
If not, probably skip.
2. Is the message clear in one sentence?
If not, it is probably too messy.
3. Is this attraction, trust, or proof?
If it fits none, skip.


4. Does this make Eyoub stronger as a brand?
If not, skip.
5. Is it fully halal and brand safe?
If not, do not use it.

1. Must have 40% of audience from USA, CA, AUS, UK
2. Must have met 0.8% engagement rate (8 likes per 1000 views)


11  THE EASIEST WAY TO THINK ABOUT CLIPPING
Prioritize clips that make the viewer think:
✓ “This guy actually gets it.”
✓ “This is different.”
✓ “This guy has standards.”
✓ “This feels real.”
✓ “I respect this.”
✓ “I need to hear more from him.”
FINAL CHECK
If the clip creates one of those reactions while staying fully halal and on-brand, it is probably a good clip.
