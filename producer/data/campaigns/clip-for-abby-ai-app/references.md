CLIPS
WHAT IS ABBY?

Abby is an AI thought companion that helps people talk through their thoughts, feelings, relationships, stress, and difficult decisions. It remembers your story and uses past conversations to give more personalized responses.
WHO IS IT FOR?
People who:
* Are dealing with relationship problems
* Feel stressed, lonely, or overwhelmed
* Are stuck on a difficult decision
* Want someone to talk to at any time
* Want to understand their thoughts and feelings better
WHAT PROBLEM DOES IT SOLVE?
A lot of people have things on their mind but don't have someone they can talk to right away.
Abby gives them a private, 24/7 space to talk things through, reflect, and get perspective without having to wait for an appointment or bother someone they know.
Simple way to think about it:
Abby = someone to talk to when you need to get your thoughts out and make sense of what's going on.
IMPORTANT: Abby is an AI companion, not a therapist and does not diagnose or treat mental health conditions.
