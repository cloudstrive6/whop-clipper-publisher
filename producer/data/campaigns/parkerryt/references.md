Campaign Focus
Create the funniest, most entertaining, and most shareable clips from Parkerr’s streams.
Every clip should answer:
“Would someone who has never seen Parkerr stop scrolling, watch this, and want to see more?”
Creative Requirements
Every submission must:
• Be vertical format
• Be at least 7 seconds long
• Have a strong opening hook
• Include Parkerr branding
• Be uploaded from a personal style page, not a branded account
• Remain live for at least 30 days
Client Assets
 Twitch stamp/branding asset (attached in email) Twitch: https://www.twitch.tv/realparkerr YouTube: https://www.youtube.com/@ParkerrLive TikTok: https://www.tiktok.com/@parkerrtv Instagram: https://www.instagram.com/real_parkerr
Campaign Strategy
Prioritize moments that are:
• Funny
• Unexpected
• Chaotic
• Emotional
• High-energy
• Reaction-based
• Easy to understand without context
Good clips make viewers instantly understand:
• Who Parkerr is
• What is happening
• Why they should keep watching

Editing Rules
Make every clip optimized for short-form.
Required:
• Hook within the first 1-3 seconds
• Remove dead time
• Keep the pacing fast
• Add captions
• Add zooms, cuts, sound effects, or other edits when they improve the clip
• Make the clip understandable without watching the full stream
Every clip must include the Parkerr Twitch stamp.


Creative Direction
Do:
 Find moments that make Parkerr look entertaining and worth following
 Choose clips that have viral potential
 Prioritize quality over quantity
Do not:
 Make Parkerr look bad
 Use misleading edits or remove context to change the meaning of a moment
 Post clips designed only to embarrass or damage Parkerr’s reputation
 Fabricate reactions, quotes, or situations
 Force controversy that is not naturally present
Duplicate Content
• Do not repost another clipper’s edit
• Do not submit near-identical edits of clips already posted
• If multiple people clip the same moment, make your version meaningfully different
