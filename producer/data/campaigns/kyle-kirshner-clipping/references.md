How To Not Get 0 Views?
--
How to warm up your account & avoid getting shadow banned:


Alright, listen up. This is one of the most important steps if you want your content to actually get views. Even if you post the best videos, if you don’t warm up your account properly, TikTok might not push them at all. Worst case? You get shadow banned, meaning your views stay stuck at 0 or super low. So let’s make sure that doesn’t happen.


“applies to all platforms”
--
1. Don’t Rush It:


* You just made a new account? Cool. But don’t post anything yet. For the first 1-3 days, just act like a regular user. Scroll through videos, like some, leave comments, and follow a few creators in your niche. This tells TikTok you're a real person, not a bot.
-
2. Stay In Your Niche:


* Only engage with content similar to what you plan to post. If you’re going to make gaming content, watch and interact with gaming videos. If you’re into finance, engage with finance creators. This helps TikTok’s algorithm understand what your account is about.
-
3. No Spammy Behavior:


* Don’t go crazy following a bunch of people or liking too many posts at once. That’s a red flag. Keep it slow, maybe follow 10-20 people a day and like a reasonable amount of videos.
-
4. Set Up Your Account Properly:


* Link an email and phone number to your account. This makes it look more legit and reduces the risk of TikTok limiting your reach.
-
5. Start Posting Smartly:
* After warming up, you can start posting. Try using trending sounds and hashtags to help your videos get pushed. Also, don’t delete underperforming videos too fast - TikTok tracks how you handle your content.
-
6. Bonus Tip: Use Stories & Replies:


* If you're also active on Instagram, use stories to engage with people. On TikTok, replying to comments helps boost engagement, which can push your videos to more people.
