3
	What to Post
	

A. In-scene moment. Drop straight into the wildest second of a scene: a reaction, a blowup, a moment that makes no sense out of context. Let it play with subtitles, then cut before it fully resolves so people go watch the episode. The scene earns the view; the full episode is the payoff.
B. Big-name moments. Show HardScope talent with other big talent whenever you can.
	

4
	How to Edit
	

1. Hook in the first 2 seconds. Lead with the wildest moment, not the setup.
2. Let the scene be the payoff. Keep the setup short and get to the moment quickly.
3. Add big, readable subtitles. Every word should be easy to follow with the sound off.
4. Keep it vertical and fast-paced. 9:16, no dead air, and at least 7 seconds long.
5. Real moments only. Episode 1 footage only. Never pair audio with a scene it isn't from, invent context, or use a thumbnail that lies about what happens. Never upload the full episode.
