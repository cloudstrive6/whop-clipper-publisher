High Priority Format
* Hook in the first 1 to 3 seconds. Start mid-sentence on her strongest line. No slow intros.
* Her real voice always wins over music. Keep music low under her.
* Lead with a strong opinion or a story beat, not a bio summary.
* The computer-screen format (clips shown on a screen) works well. Use it when the footage fits.

Overall Content Style
* The woman behind The Swan: introduce the creator, position her as the expert, not a person from the past.
* Entrepreneur lessons: strong opinions and memorable advice from her Self Made interviews. Money, women building wealth, career, building your own opportunities. Skip generic motivation.
* Immigrant story: Cuba at 5, sold Avon at 14, ran a TV station at 22. Resilience and ambition, made relatable.
* Self-made woman: agency and confidence. "Stop waiting for permission," "create your own opportunity."
* The reinvention expert: transformation as choice and growth, ties her whole philosophy together.
* Nely unfiltered: her real personality, blunt and funny. Only takes she actually says on camera. Keep her edge, end on a question so people argue. She'd rather have fun with this than lean heavy on finance, so weight toward personality over lectures.
* Her side of The Swan: she wasn't supposed to be on camera, the life coach quit, she stepped in, later got a doctorate. Her voice, her perspective.
