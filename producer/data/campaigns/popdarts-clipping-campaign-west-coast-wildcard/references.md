BEFORE YOU POST — 4 Things to Remember


1. CAMPAIGN OBJECTIVE
The goal is to make Popdarts feel big, competitive, entertaining, and impossible to scroll past across short-form social feeds. Use the provided Popdarts footage to turn the best throws, reactions, personalities, trick shots, and tournament moments into native-looking sports content.


The content should feel like it came from a sports or entertainment page — not an advertisement.

2. POST TYPE A — GAMEPLAY / COMPETITION CLIPS
Prioritize game-winning throws, clutch shots, Instant Win attempts, close finishes, comebacks, big misses, pressure moments, rivalries, celebrations, big reactions, crowd reactions, and funny competitive moments.










How to Edit It
• Open with the strongest moment.
• Hook viewers within the first 1–3 seconds.
• Cut unnecessary downtime.
• Use vertical 9:16 whenever possible.
• Keep the player, Popdart, target, score, and reaction visible.
• Add captions/subtitles when dialogue matters.
• Use short overlay text when it makes the moment easier to understand.
• Do not change scores, results, or what actually happened.


EXAMPLE HOOK TEXT
“ONE SHOT TO WIN IT ALL 😳”
“NOBODY EXPECTED THIS TO LAND”
“THE PRESSURE ON THIS THROW…”
“HE ACTUALLY CALLED THIS SHOT 🤯”
“THIS ROUND GOT OUT OF CONTROL”
“HE NEEDED THIS TO STAY ALIVE”
“WAIT FOR THE LAST THROW…”
“POPDARTS GETS SERIOUS FAST”


3. POST TYPE B — TRICK SHOTS / CRAZY MOMENTS
Use the most visually impressive Popdarts moments. These are especially useful for reaching people who do not know Popdarts yet.


What to Look For
• Wigglenobbers
• T-Bones
• Unusual bounces
• Long-distance throws
• Difficult angles
• Unexpected sticks
• Multiple crazy shots in one sequence
• Shots that surprise the player
• Anything that makes you immediately replay the video








How to Build It
• Show the payoff quickly.
• Replay the shot when useful.
• Slow motion can be used when it helps viewers understand what happened.
• Keep overlays short.
• Let reactions play out.
• Build compilations around a clear theme when enough footage exists.


EXAMPLE HOOK TEXT
“HOW DID THIS EVEN STICK?! 🤯”
“THAT BOUNCE WAS DIFFERENT.”
“ONE OF THE CRAZIEST SHOTS WE'VE SEEN”
“THIS SHOULD NOT HAVE WORKED 😭”
“THE ACCURACY IS INSANE”
“YOU HAVE TO SEE THIS IN SLOW MOTION”
