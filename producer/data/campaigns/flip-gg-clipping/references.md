Campaign Overview + Goal ​
Your mission is to drive new first-time user deposits to Flip.gg and embed the brand in the crypto and meme coin niche. This campaign is TikTok only. Place the full 5-second Flip.gg overlay, at full volume, over a viral meme/edit from the crypto and meme coin niche, and make sure Flip.gg is clearly visible. Integrate the overlay naturally so the video feels authentic and not like an ad, because clips with the overlay dropped randomly at the end will be rejected.

Use This Format + Clipping Guide ​
Find your viral clip: Search TikTok for viral meme coin and crypto space clips, such as Kimchi. Use these viral clip ideas as a starting point:

Pick the right niche: Your base video must be a viral edit/meme/video in the crypto and meme coin niche from the 2 formats above (meme edits or success/larp crypto edits).
Swap out the CS Go footage: The Format 1 examples use CS Go footage. Replace it with your crypto or meme coin clip while keeping the same format.
Add the Flip.gg overlay: Pick any overlay you like from the folder, since each one has a different voiceover. Place the full 5-second overlay over your viral meme at full volume.
Leave the overlay untouched: Do not crop, cover, blur, speed up, or heavily edit it. Flip.gg must be clearly visible.
Blend it in naturally: Work the overlay into the flow of the video. Do not just drop it randomly at the end, or the clip will be rejected.
Edit for retention and virality: Keep the whole video built to hold attention and get shared.
Post it the right way: Post on TikTok only, keep it public, use honest captions, and never use botted views or fake engagement.
Submission Checklist ​
 DO
Create original, high retention edits.
Make sure your clip is meaningfully different if inspired by another (no 1:1 copies).
Use strong hooks and pacing.
Write accurate captions and titles that reflect the actual content.
Use clean subtitles and formatting.
Prioritize quality over quantity, one great clip beats ten rushed ones.
Make content people genuinely want to watch and share.
 DON'T
Story boost or artificially inflate views.
Post low effort or low quality edits.
Use engagement bait (misleading titles, fake context, clickbait thumbnails).
Post stolen or reuploaded clips.
Spam post for quantity over quality.
Post the same or nearly identical clip more than 5 times.
Recycle repetitive content with little to no value.
Try to game the algorithm instead of making quality content.
 Consequences for repeated violations
Reduced approvals.
Lower priority for future opportunities.
Possible removal from campaigns.
