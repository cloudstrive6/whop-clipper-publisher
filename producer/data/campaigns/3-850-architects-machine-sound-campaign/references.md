Campaign Guidelines | By ClippedIn


Rules at a Glance
* Footage: the official Machine content bank, or your own edit in one of the four approved formats below.
* Sound: the official “Machine” sound from TikTok or Instagram, started at 0:43. Nothing else. 100% audible volume
* Caption: MACHINE by Architects. Out now. #architects #machine
* Banned: Other music videos, street fights, memes, movie clips, unrelated viral clips.


Campaign Overview


“Machine” is the new single from Architects. Two lanes, one sound: edits of the official music video from the content bank, and your own edits in four approved formats: dark cinematic, combat sports, gym and lifting, and gaming.
The content should feel natural to the song. Do not force unrelated clips or random reposts with music added. The content bank and the approved formats below are the ONLY APPROVED visual sources for this campaign.


Approved Song


Architects: “Machine”
TIKTOK OFFICIAL SOUND
IG REELS OFFICIAL SOUND
Start the sound at 0:43, not at the beginning of the song. Trim it in TikTok or Instagram before you post so every clip uses the same part of the track.

MACHINE by Architects. Out now. #architects #machine

Approved Formats


You must only make edits according to any of the formats below:
1. Official video edit. Cut the music video footage from the content bank to the sound from 0:43. 
2. Translated lyric edit. Use the translated cutdowns (German, Dutch, Spanish, French or Russian) that match your audience’s language. 
3. Dark cinematic edit. Dark film and anime scene edits with a cold, high contrast look: The Batman, Dune, Blade Runner 2049, Attack on Titan, Berserk, Chainsaw Man. 
4. Combat sports edit. UFC, MMA and boxing knockouts, finishes and ring walks, with the finish landing on the heaviest hit. 
5. Gym and lifting edit. Max attempt PRs, powerlifting, strongman and training montages.
6. Gaming montage edit. Elden Ring boss finishes, Call of Duty montages, Valorant aces and clutches, with the kill or final hit synced to the impact.

Important Rules
